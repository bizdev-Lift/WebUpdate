How it works

Connected in an afternoon

  1. Authorise the connection

    Log in to your store and approve the Lift Commerce connection. No developer, no plugin to maintain, no code on your storefront. Multi-store and multi-marketplace sellers connect as many channels as they need.

  2. Orders flow into one queue

    Every channel lands in a single view with addresses validated and items mapped to your package profiles. Batch by carrier, service, ship date or warehouse.

  3. Rate-shop and set automation rules

    Compare live rates across carriers on every order, or write rules that pick automatically by weight, zone, dimensions, declared value or service promise — so the cheapest compliant option gets chosen without anyone deciding.

  4. Print, track and push back

    Labels print to your existing thermal printers. Tracking numbers write back to the store and trigger your customer notifications, so buyers see the same status you do.

Where the savings actually come from

01

Big boxes cost more than you think

Oversize, additional handling and dimensional weight can add $30 to $60 to a single parcel before transportation is counted. If you ship large or awkward products, this is almost always the biggest line to attack.

02

No monthly platform fee

Most shipping platforms charge per month, per user or per label. Ours is free to use — you pay for freight, not for software seats — so the savings stay with you instead of funding a subscription.

03

A named account manager

A direct line rather than a ticket queue. They review your invoices, hunt for savings and handle claims, tracers and lost packages.
