How to connect

Prepare your Cin7 shipping workflow in four steps

  1. Review your Cin7 account and order flow

    Start by identifying your Cin7 product, sales channels and fulfillment needs. Our team can confirm supported integration features and the steps needed to connect your existing shipping operation.

  2. Set up warehouse packaging profiles

    Record the cartons, parcel weights and package dimensions your team works with each day. Accurate shipment details help improve carrier comparisons and reduce avoidable billing adjustments.

  3. Choose carrier rules for your shipments

    Review available shipping services based on destination, weight, package size and delivery expectations. Establish practical preferences that suit your products, customers and regular order volumes.

  4. Print labels and organize dispatch

    Prepare eligible orders for shipping, print labels and keep tracking details organized. Supported fulfillment updates depend on the connection configured for your Cin7 environment.

Questions

Cin7 shipping integration FAQ

Is the Cin7 shipping integration free?

Lift Commerce does not charge monthly software platform fees or per-seat licensing. You still pay the applicable freight and carrier costs for shipments handled through your account.

Can Lift Commerce work with Cin7 inventory orders?

Cin7 is listed among Lift Commerce integrations. Available order synchronization and fulfillment features depend on the Cin7 product you use and how the connection is configured.

Can I compare different carriers for Cin7 shipments?

Yes. Available shipping services can be compared using package weight, dimensions, destination and delivery requirements. Your account configuration determines which carrier options are accessible.

Can I manage orders from multiple warehouses?

Our team can review your warehouse locations and shipping process to confirm which workflows are supported. The setup depends on your Cin7 configuration and fulfillment requirements.

Does shipping tracking update in Cin7?

Supported tracking and order status updates depend on the configured Cin7 integration. Your account manager can explain the available synchronization options before you begin shipping.
