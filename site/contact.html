What happens next

So you know what you are agreeing to

No drip campaign, no sequence of chase emails. This is the whole process.

  1. We reply within one business day A person reads what you sent and answers it. If your question has a short answer, you get the short answer rather than an invitation to a call.
  2. If it warrants analysis, we ask for two documents A recent carrier invoice, ideally a full billing period, and your current rate agreement. That is enough to profile your shipping properly.
  3. You get a written scorecard in one to two business days What you pay now, what comparable volume should cost, which terms are weakest, and what to ask for. It is yours to keep whether or not you work with us.
  4. You decide what to do with it Some shippers take the analysis back to their existing carrier and renegotiate. Others move volume to our group programmes. Both outcomes are fine.

Before you send anything

The two things that make a rate analysis genuinely useful are a full billing period rather than a single label, and the rate agreement itself rather than a summary of it. If you only have one of the two, send it anyway and we will work with what you have.

If you ship anything oversize, tell us the dimensions of your most common box. That is usually where the money is.
