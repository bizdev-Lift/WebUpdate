    How it works

    How FedEx prices a large box, step by step

    1. Chargeable weight. FedEx rounds each side to the nearest inch, multiplies them and divides by the DIM divisor (139 at published rates). You pay for the higher of that figure and the actual weight. Boxes that trip additional handling for size bill at 40 lb or more; oversize boxes at 90 lb or more.
    2. Base rate. The published Ground rate for that weight and zone, less your ground discount, but never below the minimum charge ($11.99 in 2026).
    3. One additional handling charge. A box can qualify on weight (over 50 lb), dimension (longest side over 48 in, second side over 30 in, length + girth over 105 in, or over 10,368 in³) and packaging. FedEx charges only the highest one.
    4. Oversize instead. Over 96 in long, over 130 in length + girth, over 17,280 in³ or over 110 lb, the oversize charge replaces additional handling.
    5. Peak surcharges. From late September to mid-January, FedEx adds a demand surcharge to every additional handling and oversize package, highest from Nov. 23 to Dec. 27.
    6. Fuel. A weekly percentage, applied to the base rate and the surcharges.

    Common questions

    What is the FedEx Ground minimum charge in 2026?

    $11.99, the published rate for a 1 lb package to Zone 2. Many agreements set a lower minimum, which matters if you ship a lot of small, light parcels.

    Can FedEx charge more than one additional handling surcharge on a box?

    No. If a package qualifies on weight and dimension, FedEx charges the higher of the two. A 49 in box weighing 52 lb, for example, is charged the weight surcharge only.

    When do FedEx's 2026 peak surcharges apply?

    For additional handling and oversize packages, from Sept. 28, 2026 to Jan. 17, 2027. The holiday window, Nov. 23 to Dec. 27, carries the highest amounts.

    Is a better DIM divisor worth more than a bigger discount?

    Often, for light, bulky boxes. The divisor lowers the weight every rate is looked up at, so it reduces the base rate before any percentage discount. Try 139 against 225 above.
