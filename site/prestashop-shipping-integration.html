How PrestaShop orders move into fulfillment

PrestaShop keeps customer purchases, product information and delivery preferences within your store. Lift Commerce supports the shipping stage by organizing eligible order details, reviewing available carrier rates and helping your team prepare parcels.

Using PrestaShop carrier or checkout modules?

Many PrestaShop stores use additional modules to manage carrier choices, delivery zones and checkout rates. Those settings influence what customers see before purchasing and how the order is handled afterward.

Our team can review your PrestaShop version, installed modules and shipping process to confirm an appropriate connection without assuming every checkout feature will change.

How to connect

Get PrestaShop shipping ready in four steps

  1. Review your store version and connection options

    Share your PrestaShop version, current shipping modules and fulfillment needs. Your Lift Commerce account manager can confirm supported order import methods and connection requirements.

  2. Prepare package sizes and product weights

    Record common boxes, mailers and shipment weights. Accurate dimensions help your team compare carrier rates and avoid preventable adjustments, especially for products requiring additional protective packaging.

  3. Review available delivery services

    Compare shipping options according to package size, destination and customer expectations. Establish practical service preferences while keeping your existing PrestaShop checkout settings in mind.

  4. Print shipping labels and organize dispatch

    Prepare eligible orders in your shipping workspace and print labels for carrier collection. Confirm supported tracking and order status synchronization with your PrestaShop connection.

Questions

PrestaShop shipping integration FAQ

Does Lift Commerce charge monthly fees for PrestaShop shipping?

Lift Commerce does not charge monthly shipping software platform fees or per-seat licensing. Your business still pays the applicable carrier charges for each shipment.

Can I connect my PrestaShop store to Lift Commerce?

PrestaShop is listed among Lift Commerce integrations. Supported connection methods and order import features depend on your store version, installed modules and account configuration.

Can I compare carriers for PrestaShop orders?

Yes. Available carrier rates can be compared using shipment weight, package dimensions, delivery destination and required delivery speed.

Does Lift Commerce work with existing PrestaShop shipping modules?

Compatibility with existing carrier and checkout modules depends on your installed extensions and configured integration. Our team can review your store before connection.

Can tracking information sync back to PrestaShop?

Available tracking and order status synchronization depends on the configured PrestaShop connection. Your account manager can confirm supported updates before launch.
