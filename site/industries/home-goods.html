Home goods

You are not shipping furniture. You are shipping air.

Rugs, lighting, seating, decor and cookware share one economic problem: the carton is large and the contents are light. In this category dimensional weight is not one cost driver among many, it is usually the largest single line on the invoice.

Get a free rate analysis Talk to an expert

The products

Big cartons, small scale weight

Everything here bills on volume rather than weight, which is exactly the case parcel carriers price most aggressively against.

The market

Where this category sits online

Home and furniture is one of the larger online categories by spend, and one where the gap between what a customer will pay for delivery and what delivery actually costs is widest. Free-shipping expectations were set by categories with far better density.

Volume without density

Home goods generate large cartons at low weight, the worst possible combination under a dimensional weight regime.

The parcel-to-freight boundary

Larger furniture crosses out of parcel entirely. Knowing exactly where that line sits for your catalogue is worth real margin.

Damage and claims exposure

Glass, ceramics, lighting and finished surfaces make this a high-claims category, which makes packaging specification and claims handling part of the shipping cost.

Shipping profile

What actually drives your invoice

Work through a typical home goods carton and dimensional weight usually accounts for more of the bill than the base rate does.

Cost drivers in this category
DriverWhy it hits this categoryWhat controls it
Dimensional weightA 30 x 24 x 20 in carton at 28 lbs bills as 104 lbs on a 139 divisor — nearly four times its scale weight.Negotiating the divisor up, and reducing carton size, both attack this directly
Additional handling — cubicCubic size over 5,000 in³ triggers the charge. Most home goods cartons clear it easily.Right-size packaging; even an inch off each dimension moves real money
Additional handling — length and girthRug tubes, curtain rods and long shelving pass 48 in and 105 in length plus girth.Rolled versus folded packing changes the classification on rugs specifically
Residential deliveryAlmost all volume in this category is direct to a home.Unavoidable on most orders; make sure it is priced into your delivery offer
Damage claimsFragile contents plus large cartons plus multiple handling touches.Documented packaging standards and declared value; claims fail on paperwork more than merit
Freight crossoverSofas, dining tables and large case goods are not parcel shipments.LTL or partial truckload, quoted against parcel so the cheaper mode wins

Divisor first, discount second

For a catalogue this light, moving from a 139 divisor to 166 reduces the billable weight on every single oversize carton you ship. That usually beats a headline percentage discount on the base rate.
