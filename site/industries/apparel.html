Apparel

Light packages, heavy minimum charges.

Apparel is the biggest category online and the one where the published rate card fits worst. Most orders weigh under a pound, which means the carrier's minimum charge, not the weight break, is setting your price. The savings here come from mail alternatives and zone skipping, not from a bigger percentage discount.

Get a free rate analysis Talk to an expert

The products

Light, soft, and returned constantly

Almost everything in this catalogue fits a poly mailer, which is both the opportunity and the problem.

The market

Where this category sits online

Clothing is the most-purchased category online in the US, with roughly 43% of consumers buying apparel online in recent survey data, and footwear close behind. Volume is not the problem in this category. Cost per light parcel is.

The largest online category

Apparel leads US online shopping by participation, which means carriers know exactly how much of your volume they hold.

Returns are structural

Fit uncertainty makes apparel returns a permanent operating cost rather than an exception, and every return is a second shipping charge.

Under a pound is the norm

Most orders sit in the weight band where minimum charges, not weight-based rates, determine what you pay.

Shipping profile

What actually drives your invoice

The single most useful thing to understand about apparel shipping is that discounts applied to weight-based rates do very little when the minimum charge catches the package anyway.

Cost drivers in this category
DriverWhy it hits this categoryWhat controls it
Minimum chargeA heavily discounted rate on a 6 oz package is irrelevant if the minimum charge is higher than the discounted rate.Negotiate the minimum charge specifically; it is the number that governs this catalogue
Mail and alternative carriersLight parcels are exactly what postal and consolidator networks are built for.USPS commercial pricing, regional carriers and consolidators frequently beat national parcel on sub-pound packages
Zone skippingPaying zone 7 and 8 rates on every distant order when volume could be injected closer.Consolidate and line-haul to a regional injection point, then hand to a local carrier for final delivery
Dimensional weightOuterwear, boots and multi-item orders in boxes rather than mailers.Mailer-versus-box decisions made per order, not per warehouse habit
Return shippingEvery return is a second movement, usually at retail rates if handled ad hoc.Consolidated return programmes and rules-driven return labels
Peak surchargesApparel volume concentrates in exactly the weeks carriers apply peak fees.Peak surcharge terms are negotiable and are frequently left at published rates
Residential deliveryEffectively all volume is direct to consumer.Built into the offer; alternative carriers often price residential better

Three levers, in order of value

Minimum charge first, then mail and consolidator alternatives for sub-pound parcels, then zone skipping once volume justifies the line-haul. A percentage discount on the base rate is a distant fourth for this catalogue.

  1. Rate-shop postal against parcel on every order

    The cheapest network for a 6 oz mailer and a 4 lb boxed coat are rarely the same carrier. Rules that compare both on every order rather than defaulting to one.

  2. Mailer versus box, decided by rule

    Packaging choice driven by contents and dimensional weight, since in this category the box decision often costs more than the carrier decision.

  3. Service downgrade where transit allows

    Ground versus expedited chosen by destination zone and promised date, so nearby orders stop riding services they do not need.

  4. Returns as a standing programme

    Return labels issued on the cheapest compliant service automatically, with the option to consolidate returns rather than move them one at a time.
