What the connection does

What moves between Shift4Shop and Lift Commerce

Shift4Shop stays your storefront and order system. Lift Commerce takes over the shipping step: it pulls orders in, picks the carrier, prints the label and reports back.

How to connect

Connect your Shift4Shop store in an afternoon

  1. Approve the Shift4Shop connection

    Log in to your Shift4Shop store and approve Lift Commerce. No developer, no plugin to maintain and no code added to your storefront. Run more than one Shift4Shop store? Connect each one.

  2. Set up your package profiles

    Tell us the boxes you ship in. Shift4Shop items are matched to those profiles so every order arrives with accurate weight and dimensions, which is what carriers bill on.

  3. Write your carrier rules

    Choose how a carrier gets picked: by weight, zone, dimensions, declared value or delivery promise. Or rate-shop by hand on any order.

  4. Ship and let tracking flow back

    Batch-print labels by carrier, service, ship date or warehouse. Tracking numbers post to each Shift4Shop order automatically.

Automation

Rules that pick the carrier so nobody has to

Shift4Shop orders differ in weight, size and destination. Rules compare live rates and choose the cheapest service that still meets your promise to the customer, every time.

Example rules a Shift4Shop seller might set up:

  • IfThe order is light and has no delivery promise
    ThenShip the cheapest ground service on the account
  • IfThe customer paid for expedited shipping
    ThenChoose the cheapest service that still arrives on time
  • IfThe box crosses a dimensional-weight threshold
    ThenCompare carriers on billable weight, not scale weight
  • IfDeclared value is high
    ThenRequire signature and the matching coverage

Where the savings actually come from

Big boxes cost more than you think

Oversize, additional handling and dimensional weight can add $30 to $60 to a single parcel before transportation is counted. For Shift4Shop stores selling large or awkward products, this is usually the biggest line to attack.

Questions

Shift4Shop integration FAQ

Is the Shift4Shop integration free?

Yes. Every Lift Commerce integration, including Shift4Shop, is included at no charge. There is no monthly platform fee and no per-seat licensing; you pay for freight, not software.

My store was built on 3dcart. Does this work for me?

Yes. Shift4Shop is the same platform previously sold as 3dcart; it was renamed in 2021 after Shift4 acquired it. Stores that started on 3dcart connect the same way.

Do I need a developer to connect Shift4Shop?

No. You approve the connection from your store login. There is no plugin to maintain and no code added to your storefront.

Can I connect more than one Shift4Shop store?

Yes. Connect as many Shift4Shop stores as you run, plus any other marketplaces you sell on. All orders land in one queue.

Will my Shift4Shop customers receive tracking?

Yes. When a label prints, the tracking number is written back to the order in Shift4Shop, which triggers the customer notifications you already have set up.
